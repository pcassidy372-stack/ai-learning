RISK AREAS

The primary risk is the ongoing instability in CICS Region 4. Three consecutive failures with SLA breaches indicate a potentially serious underlying condition, whether resource contention, a configuration error introduced in a recent change window, or a degraded connection to dependent subsystems. Given that CICS regions support real-time transaction processing, continued failure in this area poses a direct risk to customer-facing services and could have regulatory and reputational implications if left unresolved.

A secondary risk is the JES2 failure. While isolated, JES2 is foundational to job scheduling and output management. If this failure is a leading indicator of a broader issue, the impact on batch windows and downstream processing chains could be substantial.

The aggregate success rate of 60 percent, if sustained or reported externally, could trigger scrutiny from audit, compliance, or senior leadership. This metric alone justifies an expedited response.

Fourth, issue a proactive notification to downstream application owners and business stakeholders who depend on CICS Region 4 transactions, advising them of potential service degradation.
